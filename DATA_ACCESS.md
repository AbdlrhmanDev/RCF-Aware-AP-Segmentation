# Access-controlled PRAD data

The 5,000-image research PRAD subset is available through approval by the PRAD data providers and remains subject to their copyright and data-use terms.

Official repository: https://github.com/nkicsl/PRAD

**Do not commit, release, redistribute, or link directly to private downloads of:** radiographs; segmentation masks; patient metadata; user-specific PRAD download links; image-level tables containing sensitive identifiers; and original checkpoints or frozen manifests unless an independent permission assessment authorizes them.

An email providing the authors with PRAD download access is permission to obtain/use the dataset subject to its terms; it is not necessarily blanket permission to redistribute dataset files or publication figures, and it is not an institutional ethics-exemption document.
